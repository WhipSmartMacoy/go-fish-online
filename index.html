How to play: One person creates a game and shares the code or invite link. Once everyone has joined, the creator presses Start. On your turn, click a card in your hand, click another player, then press Ask. If they have that rank, they give you all of them and you go again. If not, you "go fish" and draw a card. Collect all four of a rank to make a book. When all 13 books are made, the player with the most books wins. Keep the creator's tab open: their browser runs the game.
