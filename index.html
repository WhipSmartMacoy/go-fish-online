Room code:
Discard
How to play: On your turn, draw one card from the face-down pile or take the top discard, then pick a card from your hand to discard. Make melds: sets of 3–4 of the same rank, or runs of 3+ in a row in one suit (ace is low). Cards not in a meld are deadwood (A = 1, face cards = 10). Your melds are shown in yellow automatically. When your deadwood is 10 or less, you can knock to end the hand. 0 deadwood is Gin (+25 bonus). Your opponent can then lay off their loose cards onto your melds. If they end up with equal or less deadwood, they undercut you (+25 to them). First to 100 points wins the match.
